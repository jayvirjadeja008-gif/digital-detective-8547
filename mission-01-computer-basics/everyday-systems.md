# Task 1.3 — Computer-based systems around me

| System | Main purpose | One input | One output |
|---|---|---|---|
| ATM | Provides banking services such as cash withdrawal. | Debit card and PIN | Cash and a transaction receipt |
| Traffic signal | Controls traffic at a road intersection. | Vehicle sensor or pedestrian button | Red, yellow or green signal |
| Supermarket self-checkout | Lets customers scan and pay for items. | Barcode scan | Displayed total and payment result |

These systems receive information, process it according to programmed rules and produce a useful action or result.
